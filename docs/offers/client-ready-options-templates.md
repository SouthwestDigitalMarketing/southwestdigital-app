# Client-ready options template review

Updated: 2026-09-05

## Goal

Help a lead understand the package fit, trust the value, select only relevant add-ons, and sign and pay with minimal friction.

## Shared vocabulary

- **Package:** Maintain, Improve, or Grow.
- **Core service:** foundational recurring work included in a package.
- **Package upgrade:** included work that gives a lead a reason to choose a higher package.
- **Included service:** automatically included with no separate client selection or charge.
- **Add-on:** an elective, separately priced service the lead can select.
- **Conditional service:** shown only when the assessment makes it relevant.
- **Options template:** a saved arrangement of included services, add-ons, package assignments, visibility, pricing, and lead-facing copy.

## Review templates

The templates below are additive experiments. None replaces the current default.

### Client-ready review — Essential bookkeeping

Purpose: establish a low-friction baseline and a clear package ladder.

- All packages: Monthly Bookkeeping, Audit-Ready Document System, Secure Client Portal.
- Maintain: Standard Client Support.
- Improve and Grow: Monthly Performance Reporting, First Quarterly Review.
- Improve: Priority Client Support.
- Grow: Concierge Client Support, Monthly Financial Review.
- Receipt Capture & Matching remains staff-available but hidden from the lead by default.

### Client-ready review — Visibility & control

Purpose: show two relevant paid choices without turning the card into a service menu.

- Builds on Essential bookkeeping.
- Improve and Grow add-ons: Project Profitability Tracking and Budget & Monthly Variance Review.
- Grow upgrades: Owner & Investor KPI Scorecard and Cash Flow Review.
- Receipt Capture & Matching remains hidden unless staff recommends it.

### Client-ready review — Real estate portfolio

Purpose: make property-level value easy for an investor or operator to recognize.

- Builds on Essential bookkeeping.
- All packages: Property-Level Reporting Setup.
- Improve and Grow: Ongoing Property-Level Tracking.
- Grow: Portfolio KPI Scorecard.
- Conditional Grow inclusion: QuickBooks-to-Stessa Migration when the offer actually uses Stessa.
- Visible add-ons: Receipt Capture & Property Matching for all packages; Portfolio Budget & Variance Review for Improve and Grow.

### Client-ready review — Compliance & coordination

Purpose: test a focused offer for leads with documentation, sales-tax, and tax-preparer needs.

- Builds on Essential bookkeeping.
- Visible add-ons: Sales Tax Filing & Remittance and Receipt Capture & Matching.
- Improve and Grow inclusion: Tax-Ready Handoff & CPA Coordination.
- Registered Agent Service is intentionally excluded.

## Pricing-card review checklist

For each template, review Maintain, Improve, and Grow as if seeing the offer for the first time:

1. Can the lead explain the difference between packages in one sentence?
2. Is every service name an outcome the lead can recognize?
3. Are there no more than two or three visible add-ons on a card?
4. Does every add-on have a clear price and scope boundary?
5. Does selecting an add-on update only the eligible package total?
6. Do the higher packages feel more valuable rather than merely longer?
7. Are real-estate and platform-specific items absent when irrelevant?
8. Are support and turnaround promises operationally realistic?

## Next evaluation fields

Future review rounds should return, for every item: keep/refine/merge/reprice/conditional/retire, lead-facing name, one-sentence promise, scope boundary, billing basis, package eligibility, default visibility, margin risk, lead friction, success metric, and confidence.
