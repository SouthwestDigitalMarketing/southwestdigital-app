# Email (Zoho)

1. Inventory configured business mailboxes and their full pending queues:
   inbox, unanswered client/lead mail, drafts awaiting approval, spam review and
   newsletter review. Include old mail; do not restrict to unseen IDs or 25 rows.
2. Follow All Thing sop-email-policy, sop-inbox-sorting, sop-spam-patrol and
   sop-scan-command. Stored keep/protected-sender rules win. Read full threads.
3. Apply supported reversible folder moves and existing confirmed rules; verify
   folder and UID after each move. Never purge or unsubscribe without the
   authorization required by those procedures.
4. Link forms and genuine enquiries to existing contacts/leads, record dated
   evidence and prepare reply drafts/tasks with owner and due date. Signed
   hints remain unverified until the actual contract is checked.
5. Re-read pending queues. Drafts, unsent replies, newsletter/spam judgments
   awaiting Tom and failed folder moves remain outstanding with IDs.

Related All Thing SOPs: sop-lead-file, scan-rulebook.
